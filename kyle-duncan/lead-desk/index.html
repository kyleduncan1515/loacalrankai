          The machine runs on its own every weekday around 6:40am and 12:40pm Houston time. Each run it hunts new freight leads, researches them, writes the full outreach sequence for each one into this desk, and leaves you a brief. It never sends anything or contacts anyone. You do that from Outreach due on the Today tab.
